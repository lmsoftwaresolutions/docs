Delete Your Bill Bridge India Account and Data

App name: Bill Bridge India
Developer: LM Software Solutions

Option 1: delete your business in the app (admins)

  1. Sign in as an admin of the business. Only admins see this option.
  2. Open More, then Business settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete this business.
  4. Type the business name to confirm, then tap Delete for ever.
Warning: all bills, parties, items and money records of the business are removed from the app for everyone who uses it. You cannot undo this in the app.

Option 2: ask us to erase everything permanently

Deleting a business in the app removes it from view. To have the data permanently erased from our servers, or to delete your own login (name, email address, phone number), email us:

  1. Send an email to info@lmsoftwaresolutions.com from the address registered on your account, with the subject Delete my Bill Bridge India data.
  2. Include your name, business name and phone number so we can find the account.
  3. We will confirm your identity, erase the data and reply within 30 days.

If you are staff and not an admin, ask your business admin to delete the business, or email us to remove only your own login. Your business keeps the records you created.

What is deleted

  • Business data: parties, items, stock, bills, payments, ledgers, company details and logo (removed from the app when an admin deletes the business; permanently erased on request).
  • Your user login, name, email address and phone number (on request by email).
  • Your sign-in sessions and device unlock settings.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Invoices and ledger entries that must be retained under Indian GST and income-tax rules, until the legally required period ends. If a staff login is deleted, entries they created stay in the business's records without personal contact details.
  • Backups are overwritten on their normal schedule, within 35 days.
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
