Delete Your Bill Bridge India Account and Data

App name: Bill Bridge India
Developer: LM Software Solutions

The app does not yet have a delete button. To delete your account or data, email us:

  1. Send an email to info@lmsoftwaresolutions.com from the address registered on your account, with the subject Delete my Bill Bridge India account.
  2. Include your name, company name and phone number so we can find the account.
  3. We will confirm your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days, then reply to confirm.
Company accounts: if you are the company owner, deletion removes the whole company's data for all its users. If you are a staff user, only your own user login is removed, and your company keeps the records you created.

What is deleted

  • Your user login, name, email address and phone number.
  • Your sign-in sessions and device unlock settings.
  • For a full company deletion: parties, items, stock, invoices, payments, ledgers, company details and logo.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Invoices and ledger entries that the company must retain under Indian GST and income-tax rules, until the legally required period ends. Where a staff user's login is deleted, entries they created stay in the company's records without personal contact details.
  • Backups are overwritten on their normal schedule, within 35 days.
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
