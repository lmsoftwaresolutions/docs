Privacy Policy

App name: Bill Bridge India
Developer: LM Software Solutions
Effective date: 9 November 2026

1. Overview

Bill Bridge India is a billing, stock and ledger app for businesses. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. The app works with an account on your company's Bill Bridge India software; the business that owns the account controls its business records.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account details: name, email address and phone number you or your company administrator enter.
  • Business records you create: parties (customers and suppliers), items and stock, invoices, payments, ledgers and company details such as GSTIN and address.
  • Company logo, only if you choose to upload one.
  • Item photos, only if you choose to add them.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the service, such as sign-in sessions and server logs.

3. How We Use Information

  • To sign you in and show you the records your role is allowed to see.
  • To create, store and share invoices and reports.
  • To keep the service secure and fix faults.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Your data is stored on servers run by LM Software Solutions. It is shown only to users of your own company account, according to their role. It is shared outside only if you share an invoice yourself, or if the law requires it. When you send a bill or statement by WhatsApp or e-mail from the app, it passes through WhatsApp (Meta) or our e-mail provider to reach the person you send it to. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

5. Device Permissions

  • Camera: to scan item barcodes, and to take item photos if you choose. Item photos are uploaded and kept with the item. Barcode scans are not stored.
  • Photos: only the pictures you pick for your company logo or your items.
  • Bluetooth: to send bills to a receipt printer you have paired. The app does not use your location.
  • Fingerprint / face unlock: to unlock the app on your phone. Biometric data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. Saved invoices and ledger entries are never edited or deleted one by one inside the app, because accounting and tax rules require a complete record; mistakes are fixed by returns or corrections. When an admin deletes the business, all its data is removed from the app for every user straight away. Permanent erasure is described on the account deletion page. To request deletion, see the account deletion page.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access is limited by user role. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is for business use by adults and does not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
