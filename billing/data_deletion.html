This page explains how an account owner can request deletion of an account and associated data from the billing service. Confirm the instructions and processing timelines against the Service's actual account-management process before publishing this page.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to info@lmsoftwaresolutions.com from the address associated with the account.
  2. Use the subject line “Billing account and data deletion request.”
  3. Include the account email and business name. Do not email passwords, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive credentials.
  4. Only an account owner or an authorized representative may request deletion. We may ask for information needed to verify authority.

What Happens Next

After verifying the request, LM Software Solutions will explain any applicable consequences, process deletion of data that is eligible for removal, and confirm when the request is complete. Publish a specific expected processing time here once the operational timeline has been confirmed.

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained where required or permitted by law, including accounting and tax records, records needed to resolve disputes, and information needed to protect the Service. Any retained information will be restricted to the applicable purpose and kept only as long as necessary.

Backups and Connected Services

Copies in backups may remain until they are removed through the Service's normal backup lifecycle. Data already sent to third-party services or recipients may need to be deleted through those services or recipients separately. Confirm and document the applicable providers and backup deletion timeline before publication.

Before You Request Deletion

Account deletion may remove access to invoices, customer and supplier records, inventory, and other business information. Export or retain any records you need before submitting a request, subject to your legal obligations.
